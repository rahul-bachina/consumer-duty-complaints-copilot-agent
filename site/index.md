## Who it is for

Complaints teams, Consumer Duty champions, compliance and conduct-risk staff, and data protection officers in UK banks, insurers, payment and e-money firms, consumer credit firms, and claims management companies. Solution architects can read the Copilot Studio and Microsoft Foundry build notes before anyone connects a tenant.

## What it does

It turns one inbound complaint into a structured draft: complaint or not, product, root cause, a severity for a person to change, vulnerability characteristics with the words that prompted them, DISP dates from Python, a Consumer Duty outcome to accept or change, and an acknowledgement that must not be sent as it stands.

## What it does not do

It never decides a complaint outcome. It does not uphold, reject, set redress, send a letter, write a Dynamics 365 case, refer a complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service, or label a customer vulnerable.
